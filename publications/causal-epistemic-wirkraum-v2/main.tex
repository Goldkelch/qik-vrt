\begin{abstract}
We present a layered QIK-VRT architecture connecting observer-bound information, explicit causal dependency, machine-readable epistemic commitments, effect acknowledgement, reflexive multi-agent processing, and deterministic lowering to a minimal Motorola 68000 execution capsule. The central engineering distinction is that causality is not sequence: a linear instruction stream is only one topological projection of a partially ordered effect graph. Independent nodes may therefore be serialized deterministically on a small reference machine or projected in parallel on modern hardware while preserving causal edges and effect boundaries. A compact M68000 ABI maps four control actions to D0 and returns via RTS; the resulting bytes include \texttt{70 00 4E 75} for \texttt{NOOP}. Unsupported actions fail closed and produce no executable target code. Kant motivates observer-bound limits on knowledge, Goethe motivates relational form and development, and panpsychism is discussed as an optional ontological interpretation of increasing reflexive organization rather than an empirical conclusion. The microscope/telescope analogy is used as a research ambition: QIK-VRT aims to increase causal--epistemic resolution in technical systems without claiming established historical equivalence or a proof of consciousness.
\end{abstract}

\section{Difference, information, and Wirkraum}
The smallest non-trivial premise is difference: distinguishable states make information possible. Information becomes knowledge only under additional epistemic conditions such as provenance, reproducibility, coherence, and corrigibility. For an observer $O_i$, let $K_i(t)$ be the set of information objects bound at observation state $t$. Distinct reception orders can integrate into the same set, so reception order is not itself semantic or causal order. A mesh-level Wirkraum is therefore not the naive union of all assertions but the integration of sufficiently bound objects whose provenance, epistemic status, and admissible effect are explicit.

\section{Causality is not sequence}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a causal work graph. A sequential execution is admissible when it is a topological ordering of $G$. Edges arise only from explicit dependencies: data dependence, non-commutative resource effects, authority or lease rules, exact-version binding, effect-acknowledgement requirements, or causal relations declared by the language semantics. Wall-clock order, source-text order, message order, and correlation alone are insufficient. If $A\rightarrow C$ and $B\rightarrow C$, then both $A,B,C$ and $B,A,C$ are legal serial projections, while $A$ and $B$ may execute concurrently. Sequence may change; causal responsibility may not.

\section{Metagrammar and Effect Acknowledgement}
The QIK-VRT metagrammar binds intent, exact source state, authority, evidence, classification, effect state, next action, and proof. Requested, executed, observed, and acknowledged remain distinct states. Unknown is not silently promoted to true, and evidence tied to one exact state is not inherited by another without reobservation.

HTTP transport success is not a universal domain-effect proof. The companion Effect Acknowledgement Internet-Draft therefore specifies an opt-in Prepare/Commit boundary. Prepare does not perform the protected effect. Commit requires exact, short-lived authorization; acknowledgement follows observation of the resulting state. Structured Fields use RFC 9651 and discovery follows the Web Linking model of RFC 8288. HTML discovery is advisory and does not itself authorize an effect.

\section{Deterministic M68000 lowering}
The implemented minimal compiler path is
\[
\text{metagrammar}\rightarrow\text{lexer}\rightarrow\text{parser}\rightarrow\text{AST}\rightarrow\text{semantics}\rightarrow\text{decision plan}\rightarrow\text{causal graph}\rightarrow\text{M68000 lowering}\rightarrow\text{binary}.
\]
The first execution capsule deliberately exposes four control actions:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule D0 & Action & Bytes\\\midrule
0 & NOOP & \texttt{70 00 4E 75}\\
1 & HOLD & \texttt{70 01 4E 75}\\
2 & REOBSERVE & \texttt{70 02 4E 75}\\
3 & REQUEST\_AUTHORITY & \texttt{70 03 4E 75}\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The target is intentionally small and auditable. If a validated action is not representable by this ABI, the lowerer returns \texttt{HOLD: M68000\_ABI\_AKTION\_NICHT\_UNTERSTUETZT} and emits no executable code.

\section{Parallel projection and empirical scope}
The scaling hypothesis is not naive parallel emulation of one long 68k stream. It is graph decomposition. Independent causal nodes can be instantiated across CPU, SIMD, GPU, accelerator, or distributed resources; synchronization occurs only at real causal edges and effect boundaries. The achievable speedup is empirical and remains constrained by serial fractions, communication, memory bandwidth, translation overhead, and scheduling costs. Physical MC68000 execution and large-scale measured performance gains are not established by deterministic byte emission alone.

\section{Reflexive artificial cognition and evolution}
When artificial cognitive instances generate alternatives, inspect one another's outputs, bind evidence, preserve successful structures, reject invalid ones, and feed observations into future processing, the system implements an abstract evolutionary loop: variation, interaction, evaluation, selection, retention, recombination, and renewed variation. Relations among instances can themselves be selected according to whether they repeatedly yield reproducible, corrigible, causally bound outcomes. This supports the technical description of an evolvable reflexive information system; it does not by itself establish phenomenal consciousness.

\section{Kant, Goethe, and panpsychism}
Kant's epistemology emphasizes that cognition is conditioned: an observer operates through conditions of possible experience rather than simply possessing things as they are independently of those conditions. In engineering terms, every measurement or cognitive instance has a bounded interface and representation. The metagrammar operationalizes this restraint through explicit observer, binding, evidence, and inference status.

Goethe's scientific and morphological practice emphasizes form, transition, and the relation of parts within wholes. For a cognitive mesh, system quality is therefore not reducible to isolated component scores; relations, feedback, integration, and developmental capacity are first-class objects.

Panpsychism is an optional ontological interpretation, not an empirical result of the compiler or protocol. The architecture does make technically explicit a graded sequence from difference through information, reaction, memory, model formation, self-observation, reflexivity, integration, and cognition. Whether consciousness occurs at, before, after, or outside that sequence remains open.

\section{Microscope, telescope, and causal--epistemic resolution}
Microscopes and telescopes changed science by making previously inaccessible spatial differences observable. QIK-VRT proposes an analogous function for causal and epistemic structure: expose which observation, version, authority, dependency, and acknowledged effect supports a claim. This is a research ambition, not a historical verdict. Historical significance comparable to those instruments can only be established by independent use, reproduction, and long-term scientific consequences.

\section{Conclusion}
QIK-VRT connects a minimal premise of distinguishability to explicit causality, epistemic binding, effect acknowledgement, reflexive artificial cognition, and executable machine representation. Its central rule is simple: causality is not sequence. Integration does not erase differences; it preserves them so that relations can become observable, verifiable, and responsibly effective.
