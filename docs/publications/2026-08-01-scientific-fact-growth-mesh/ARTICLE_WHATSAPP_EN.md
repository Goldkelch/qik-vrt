# QIK-VRT: A causality mirror for verifiable knowledge

*Read-aloud version in English*

By Ingolf Lohmann.

*1. The basic idea*

Computers can search enormous quantities of text and data. Yet the most
important question often remains unanswered:

Why should a particular statement be considered reliable?

QIK-VRT therefore stores more than an answer. It binds the path behind it:
source, observation, measurement method, transformation, hypothesis, proof,
counterevidence, decision and observed effect.

This is why a QIK-VRT repository can be called a *causality mirror*.

The mirror is not the world. It shows which connection is established, which
is assumed and which is still open.

*2. Six different epistemic states*

QIK-VRT does not use only “true” and “false”.

First: *Formally proved.* A precisely named theorem was checked by the Lean
kernel in a precisely named model.

Second: *Empirically evidenced.* A bounded observation is linked to method,
calibration, uncertainty and provenance.

Third: *Source-bound.* An identified source contains the statement. This does
not mean that the system confirms its truth.

Fourth: *Normative.* The statement is a rule, definition or value judgment.

Fifth: *Interpretative.* The statement is an accountable interpretation.

Sixth: *Open.* A necessary proof, experiment or bridge is missing.

This prevents a measurement from being treated like a mathematical proof, or
a fluent conjecture from silently becoming a fact.

*3. What has actually been machine-proved*

Lean 4.19.0 checked 21 theorems in the finite QIK-VRT model. No proof gaps and
no project-specific axioms were observed.

Existing knowledge objects are preserved by additive extension.

Mesh union is commutative, associative and idempotent. At this object layer,
delivery order and duplicate delivery do not alter the final set.

Replicas converge when they receive the same updates and use the same policy.

Existing reasoning paths and explicitly stored conflicts remain findable.

Exact novelty is relative to the examined corpus. It does not prove worldwide
scientific priority.

The same event trace can be compatible with different physical causes.
Temporal order alone does not prove causation.

In the model, real digital-twin actuation requires a qualified observation and
a separate effect acknowledgement.

A proposal-only analysis never authorizes an external effect by itself.

There is also a formal counterexample: an empty corpus answers no query. The
structure alone therefore cannot guarantee an answer to every conceivable
question.

*4. A real-world example*

A sensor reports 94 degrees. A model predicts a failure. An assistant
recommends shutdown. A human confirms. The actuator responds. Later, the
temperature falls.

A normal log may show only the sequence. The causality mirror also asks:

Which sensor? Which calibration and uncertainty? Which software and model
version? Which data supported the prediction? Was shutdown merely recommended
or actually executed? Did the actuator acknowledge it? Which feedback
measurement supports the effect? Could another cause explain the cooling?

This turns a story into an inspectable graph.

Even that graph does not automatically prove physical causation. An
intervention, a control group, an identification assumption or a validated
physical model may still be required.

*5. Analogue measurement and control*

The analogue world can be connected too.

A physical quantity becomes digital data through a sensor, transfer function,
sampling, quantisation and timestamping.

QIK-VRT can bind the reading, device identity, calibration parameters,
uncertainty model, sampling rate and analysis code.

In control engineering, setpoint, state estimate, controller output, actuator
acknowledgement and observed plant response remain separate.

Communications engineering adds framing, ordering, error detection, latency
and channel capacity.

A formal theorem about a finite message does not replace a real channel test.
It identifies exactly which part is proved and which part must still be
measured.

*6. Digital twins*

A digital twin connects a physical object to a running model.

Failures often arise at the interfaces: wrong units, an outdated model,
missing calibration, a data gap, or a recommendation mistaken for an executed
action.

QIK-VRT turns these interfaces into inspectable edges. That supports root-cause
analysis, maintenance, validation, collaboration and audit. It does not
replace plant-specific safety validation.

*7. Why the quantum level matters*

A quantum experiment has a long classical chain. A circuit is specified. A
compiler translates it. A backend has a particular calibration. Measurement
shots are stored and analysed statistically.

QIK-VRT can bind circuit, compiler, backend, calibration, shots, analysis,
null model and claim by content.

Models without a fixed global causal order can also be documented without
prematurely claiming a communication channel into the past.

The value of the quantum-causality level lies in separating mathematical
model, experimental arrangement, observed statistics and physical
interpretation.

VRT emergence, the quantum-to-classical limit, a physical retrocausal bridge
and a real QPU end-to-end channel are not proved here.

*8. A growing distributed knowledge tree*

Every admitted object has a content identity. Repositories can merge their
object sets stably. Old nodes are not silently rewritten. Corrections and
refutations become new nodes that refer back.

Contradictions remain visible with source, time and method.

Search can therefore ask more than which words look alike:

Which claims depend on this measurement? Which version was kernel-checked?
Which counterevidence contests a claim? Which answer has a complete evidence
path? Where is calibration missing, where an experiment, and where a
definition?

An answer can be returned as a small, verifiable reasoning graph.

*9. Does this make people and machines smarter?*

The architecture can support better retrieval and more transparent arguments.

Whether every person or cognitive system actually improves remains open. That
requires measurable learning goals, comparison groups, preregistered tasks,
privacy safeguards and replicated studies.

QIK-VRT can manage the evidence for such a study. It cannot replace the study.

*10. Value for mathematics, physics and computing*

Mathematicians obtain a versioned proof graph. Definition, premises, exact
theorem, library version and kernel receipt remain connected.

Physicists can distinguish raw data, apparatus, calibration, analysis code,
uncertainty and competing models.

Computer science and communications engineering gain a deterministic evidence
and merge protocol.

Measurement, control and digital twins gain an auditable loop from sensor to
observed effect.

Quantum computing gains a common provenance layer for classical and quantum
components.

*11. Technical power needs verifiable responsibility*

Analysis, review, repository merge, Zenodo deposit, IETF submission and real
actuation are separate effects.

A proof or classification must not automatically publish or move a machine.

This separation also supports AI audits. A system should be able to show which
data and model version produced an output, which uncertainty was known, who
approved it and which effect was observed.

That creates audit readiness. It is not automatic EU AI Act certification or
legal advice.

*12. The precise conclusion*

QIK-VRT does not automatically know everything.

But in the declared finite model it is proved that knowledge can grow with
typed status, conflicts can be preserved, mesh replicas can merge
deterministically, and external effects can be authorized separately.

Universal truth, global scientific novelty, complete language-to-Lean
automation, answers to every question and a physical quantum or retrocausal
bridge are not claimed.

The causality mirror does not replace the world. It makes the path of our
knowledge visible enough for people and machines to inspect, contest and
improve together.

Q.E.D. — within the explicitly declared scope.

Ingolf Lohmann.
