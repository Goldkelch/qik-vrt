# Wikipedia readiness and neutral draft boundary

## Current disposition

A standalone Wikipedia article about QIK-VRT or Ingolf Lohmann's retrocausality architecture is **not publication-ready** from the present evidence bundle.

Wikipedia requires verifiability from reliable, preferably independent secondary sources; it does not accept original research or a synthesis based only on an inventor's own paper, repository, Zenodo deposits, or Internet-Draft. The current source set establishes public primary sources and exact technical artifacts, but it does not yet establish substantial independent secondary coverage of the specific QIK-VRT research contribution.

## Conflict-of-interest disclosure

Ingolf Lohmann is the originator and Product Owner of QIK-VRT. Any Wikipedia proposal should disclose that connection and should be submitted as a neutral draft or talk-page request rather than directly installing promotional claims into article space.

## What may become usable

A neutral article could be considered after independent peer-reviewed papers, substantial independent science journalism, or comparable reliable secondary analyses discuss QIK-VRT in depth. Such sources would need to evaluate the work rather than merely reproduce press material or repository metadata.

## Safe present action

Preserve a source dossier containing:

- independent secondary coverage when it appears;
- peer-review or editorial decisions;
- citations by unaffiliated researchers;
- independent reproductions or failed reproductions;
- precise distinctions between formal proof, software evidence, physical interpretation, and open channel tests.

No Wikipedia edit or article publication is claimed by this package.
