ABSTRACT:
Delayed-choice quantum erasers, delayed entanglement swapping, entanglement between timelike-separated photons, and phononic quantum erasers establish real, reproducible, temporally extended quantum correlations. A later measurement condition can determine how already registered events are partitioned into correlated subensembles. Within standard quantum mechanics, the unsorted local earlier data remain independent of the later setting; controllable backward signalling does not follow. Time-symmetric and retrocausal model classes nevertheless remain scientifically serious because they may use both initial and final boundary conditions in the description of a quantum history.

QIK-VRT transfers this temporal and correlational structure into an explicitly defined computational state space. It separates host-causal order, virtual temporal order, semantic assignment, and evidential order; proves future-indexed addressing, relative message overtaking, reverse reconstruction from complete receipts, and the absence of a host effect before its host cause under an acyclic host order. This establishes scalable computational retrograde ordering, later-conditioned semantics, and provenance-bound reverse reconstruction as real technical capabilities.

The paper further specifies a retrocausally prepared communication infrastructure and a preregistered intervention test. An operational backward channel requires a later freely generated input to change the distribution of an earlier irreversibly sealed outcome, exclusion of forward leakage, and independently replicated positive channel capacity.
