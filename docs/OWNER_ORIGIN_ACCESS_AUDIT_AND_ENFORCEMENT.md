# Owner origin, access, independent audit and fail-closed enforcement

## Bound origin

The QIK-VRT concepts, distinctions, scientific and technical direction, architecture requirements, Product-Owner decisions and corrections are attributed to **Ingolf Lohmann** as the human origin principal and Product Owner. AI systems, compilers, runners, repositories and mesh nodes are separately recorded tools for assistance, formalization, implementation, verification and materialization.

Tool participation does not erase human provenance. It also does not justify a blanket claim that every generated byte is automatically a copyright-protected human work. Statutory copyright, contractual output rights and data-protection rights remain artifact-, contract- and jurisdiction-specific.

## Rights layers remain distinct

```text
COPYRIGHT
!= DATA_PROTECTION_AND_PRIVACY
!= HUMAN_RIGHTS
!= CONTRACT_AND_LICENSE
!= ACCESS_EXPORT_AND_RECONSTRUCTION
```

The German Basic Law is retained as an owner-declared normative orientation. The categorical imperative is retained as an ethical heuristic. Neither is silently promoted into a universal legal substitute for all jurisdictions.

A user interface translated into 47 natural languages does not create legal validity in 47 countries, eliminate defences, establish a contract, transfer rights or prove compliance.

## Owner-access invariant

For every artifact created by or for the origin principal, persistence requires more than byte storage:

```text
PERSISTED
= FINDABLE
AND OPENABLE
AND EXPORTABLE
AND INTEGRITY_VERIFIABLE
AND PROVENANCE_BOUND
AND RECONSTRUCTABLE
```

A chat window, closed pull request, expiring Actions artifact or undocumented proprietary format may not be the only access path. A third-party platform cannot be forced by this repository to remain available forever; therefore the repository contract requires an owner-accessible, durable and open or fully documented representation whenever an artifact enters QIK-VRT custody.

```text
ACCESS_FAILURE
= TECHNICAL_ACCESS_DEFECT_TO_REPAIR

ACCESS_FAILURE
!= LOSS_OF_CONTENT
!= LOSS_OF_PROVENANCE
!= TRANSFER_OF_RIGHTS
!= REQUIREMENT_TO_RECREATE_THE_WORK
```

## Authority, Mirror and mesh hierarchy

Authority, Mirror and other mesh nodes preserve role-local identity:

```text
AUTHORITY != MIRROR != MESH_NODE
```

Copying a tree does not transfer canonical Authority. A bot commit does not replace the human origin principal. Every role must still preserve owner access and provenance.

## Independent verification

An independent auditor may verify exact bytes, provenance, role-local identity, declared license and the access contract without receiving owner credentials. Audit is read-only and evidence-bounded.

```text
DETECTABLE_PROVENANCE != PROVED_INFRINGEMENT
AUDIT_RESULT != LEGAL_JUDGMENT
```

## License-evidence state machine

The executable classifier admits only:

```text
NO_BYTE_IDENTITY
PROVENANCE_UNVERIFIED
LICENSE_SCOPE_UNBOUND
LICENSE_COMPATIBLE
POTENTIAL_VIOLATION_UNVERIFIED
EVIDENCED_BREACH_CANDIDATE_INTERNAL_ONLY
```

The final state is still an internal evidence candidate. It is not a public accusation and does not authorize a notice, payment demand, invoice, damages assertion, regulatory complaint or court filing.

External legal action requires exact byte identity, provenance, license or contract version, actor and conduct evidence, applicable jurisdiction, authorization evidence, causal loss evidence when damages are claimed, and a separate exact decision by Ingolf Lohmann or authorized counsel.

## Audio-source privacy

The two Product-Owner recordings are bound by filename, file identifier, byte count, duration and SHA-256. Raw audio and verbatim transcripts are not committed. Offline ASR output is treated as fallible input to a semantic binding, never as verbatim proof.
