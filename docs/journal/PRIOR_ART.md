## QIK-VRT candidate distinction

The narrower candidate novelty is the combination of:

- article-level machine-readable claim classification;
- direct binding from claims to primary evidence;
- executable repository audit producing PASS/BLOCK;
- effect-oriented audit semantics for AI/tool actions;
- adapters for multiple AI coding/agent systems;
- immutable historical article versions enforced by repository CI;
- stable canonical URLs plus explicit correction lineage;
- an interactive evidence browser rendered from the same claim registry;
- explicit separation of formal mathematics, empirical physics, law, ethics, opinion and spiritual interpretation.
