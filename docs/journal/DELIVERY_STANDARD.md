# QIK-VRT Journal delivery standard

Publication and email delivery are separate effects.

1. Use an established authenticated sender identity.
2. Send individually or in small relationship-based groups; do not use blind bulk delivery to unrelated recipients.
3. Never bypass or evade recipient spam/security controls.
4. A hard bounce closes that address until a corrected address is independently obtained.
5. Record delivery outcome separately from content publication.
6. Prefer one stable article URL over duplicating the full article in repeated outbound messages.
7. For institutional recipients, prefer published editorial/submission channels and named professional contacts.
8. Maintain deduplication so unchanged material is not repeatedly sent to the same recipient.
9. A successful send is not a successful delivery; delivery is not reading; reading is not acceptance.
10. Commercial mailing infrastructure, if later used, must use lawful sender identification, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, bounce handling and applicable consent/anti-spam rules.

No QIK-VRT mechanism is intended to defeat recipient-side filtering.
