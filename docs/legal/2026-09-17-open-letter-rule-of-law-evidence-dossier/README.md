# Open Letter / Rule-of-Law Evidence Dossier – 2026-09-17

This publication carrier binds a personal open letter by Ingolf Lohmann to a structured evidence dossier and to the existing QIK-VRT repository evidence boundary.

## Purpose

The bundle is intended for **independent legal and legal-scientific examination**. It does not ask readers to adopt the author's allegations as adjudicated facts and does not assign guilt. It provides a reproducible entry point for lawyers, legal scholars, human-rights institutions and competent authorities to distinguish:

`self-report → primary evidence → witness evidence → inference/hypothesis → official record → legal assessment`

## Legal/epistemic boundary

Publication or persistence of this bundle establishes only that the specified bytes and statements were published. It does **not** establish:

- that any named or described person committed an offence;
- that any religion or worldview bears collective responsibility;
- that any police action was unlawful;
- that any allegation against the author was knowingly false;
- that a prediction platform caused any denunciation or attack;
- that the author or another person has a legally adjudicated victim status;
- or any judicial conclusion about guilt, liability, damages or remedy.

Those are questions for evidence-based investigation and the competent independent legal procedures.

## Requested downstream work

Legal researchers and counsel are invited to use the dossier to:

1. obtain and bind primary records and case numbers;
2. preserve original digital evidence and provenance;
3. take independent witness statements;
4. reconstruct each alleged police intervention and its legal basis;
5. separate German and French jurisdictional questions;
6. test alleged religious/worldview motivation per concrete event;
7. test or falsify alleged platform-mediated causal chains;
8. identify limitation periods, procedural deadlines, available remedies and possible claims;
9. produce a legal assessment only after the factual record is sufficiently established.

## Archive strategy

The intended Zenodo object is a **publication/document bundle**, not a judicial finding. Zenodo persistence should be treated as identity/fixity evidence for the archived files, not as peer review, factual validation or legal endorsement.
